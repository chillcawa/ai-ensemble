# Code of Conduct

AI Ensemble contributors are expected to keep project spaces constructive, specific, and safe.

- Discuss work and ideas without harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish credentials, private conversations, or identifying information.
- Give technical criticism with evidence and address behavior rather than identity.
- Stop unwanted contact when asked.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban accounts when necessary to protect the project community. Report urgent security or privacy issues through the private process in `SECURITY.md`; report other conduct issues privately to the repository maintainers when possible.
