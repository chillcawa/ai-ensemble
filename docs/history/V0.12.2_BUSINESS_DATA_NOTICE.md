# AI Ensemble v0.12.2 — Business data notice

The Preview notice now supports legitimate workplace use without implying that
all input is indiscriminately disclosed.

## Clarified data flow

- The question, enabled Context, and required conversation history are sent
  directly from the device to each selected provider's official API.
- AI Ensemble has no proprietary relay server in this build.
- Provider retention and training behavior still depends on the user's plan,
  contract, and provider-side settings.

## Workplace guidance

- Work data may be used when the user's organization and provider agreement
  permit external-AI processing.
- Personal or confidential data should be minimized and, where appropriate,
  anonymized or masked.
- The notice no longer applies an unconditional ban to confidential,
  personal, or unpublished materials.

The acknowledgement storage key was advanced to `trial-notice-v2`, ensuring
existing testers see the materially revised notice once.
