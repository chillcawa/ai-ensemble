# AI Ensemble v0.13.1 — Provider slider

The nine provider cards now render as a horizontal slider instead of shrinking
all cards into the available width.

- Each card keeps a readable 248px base width.
- Left/right controls move by roughly three quarters of the visible strip.
- Native horizontal scrolling and touchpad gestures remain available.
- Scroll snapping keeps card boundaries aligned.
- The toolbar shows enabled/total providers and the current leading card.
- Narrow screens show one comfortably sized card at a time instead of a tall
  nine-card column.

This changes presentation only. Provider enablement, saved keys, model choices,
and broadcast behavior are unchanged.
